The Contract

Every track implements these six functions. The signatures stay the same; the internals evolve.

load_data(config) → data
clean_data(data, config) → clean_data
analyze(clean_data, config) → results
plot(clean_data, results, config) → figures
export_results(clean_data, results, figures, config) → paths
self_check() → None
Your Journey

How it works

One GitHub repo for 2 years. Weekly Colab notebooks guide you through each concept, then you apply it to your project.

Year 1

CP1 takes you from zero to v1 — a working pipeline in pure Python. CP2 makes it robust with validation, testing, config, and packaging.

Year 2

OOP refactors your codebase into a clean architecture with classes, patterns, and plugins. DSA optimizes performance with efficient algorithms and data structures.

A typical week

  • Core notebook — learn the concept
  • Studio notebook — apply it to your project
  • Universal Check — automated validation
  • Homework — practice and reinforce

What if you fall behind?

The core notebook is must-pass. If you miss it, you can use a recovery ticket (3 per semester) to catch up. Beyond that, there is a rescue lane with guided remediation.

Track switching

Explore freely during weeks 1–2. Confirm your track by week 3. Locked after that.

Grading

  • 50% weekly deliverables
  • 20% quizzes / oral
  • 20% demo
  • 10% habits

AI policy

You may use AI tools, but you must be able to explain everything in your code. Disclose all AI usage.
